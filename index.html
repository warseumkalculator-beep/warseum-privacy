Privacy Policy

WarSeum Kalculator

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Data Collection

WarSeum Kalculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data or user data.

All calculations and simulations are performed locally on the user's device.

The application does not require a user account and does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, location services, camera access, microphone access, contacts, or other sensitive device permissions.

Internet Access

WarSeum Kalculator does not require an Internet connection for its core functionality.

The application may contain links to external websites, such as Tipeee. When a user chooses to open such a link, it is opened in the device's external web browser.

Any information processed by external websites is governed by their respective privacy policies and is not collected or controlled by WarSeum Kalculator.

Local Processing

Simulation parameters and calculations are processed locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer.

Children's Privacy

WarSeum Kalculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the application's data practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
